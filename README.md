# Andalusite Theme

Andalusite is a rare and fascinating gemstone, prized for its remarkable pleochroism, displaying a spectrum of colors—earthy browns, greens, and reds—depending on the angle of light. Named after the Spanish region of Andalusia, where it was first discovered in the 19th century, this aluminum silicate mineral embodies resilience and versatility. Its unique ability to shift hues inspires a dynamic yet grounded coding theme, perfect for developers seeking a balanced and visually engaging workspace.

Found in regions such as Brazil, Sri Lanka, and Australia, Andalusite’s warm, multifaceted tones evoke a sense of stability and creativity. This theme captures the gemstone’s distinctive aesthetic, offering a harmonious and inspiring environment for coding.
